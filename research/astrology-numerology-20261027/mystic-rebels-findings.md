## Actual numerical and semantic structure

`placements.py:9–15` calculates Whole Sign house number, associates the natural house sequence Aries through Pisces with Field/True Placement, retains actual astronomical sign as Tone, and sets Pulse by same-element decan: `(sign + 4 * decan_index) % 12`. Degree is geometric position; Spark remains null. Thus **house number, sign index, degree, decan number, Life Path and PMEI count are different variables even when their printed digits coincide**. A seventh-house planet is not automatically a numerological7, nor is the ninth house automatically a numerological9. [Placement implementation](https://github.com/olsonan26/mystic-rebels-chart-generator/blob/3d83db75c30b26d9b068576d84ec49554822fd8c/mystic_rebels/placements.py#L9-L15).

The natural house Field and actual sign expression are explicitly distinguished; the natural house ruler is not automatically the outer-wheel Delegate. Modern ruler names are available, but the full Delegate graph and its condition are missing. [House/Field distinction](https://github.com/olsonan26/mystic-rebels-chart-generator/blob/3d83db75c30b26d9b068576d84ec49554822fd8c/data/knowledge/interpretive_inventory.json#L505), [Delegate condition](https://github.com/olsonan26/mystic-rebels-chart-generator/blob/3d83db75c30b26d9b068576d84ec49554822fd8c/data/knowledge/interpretive_inventory.json#L1026).

## Deeper integration opportunities

### 1. Match the role of a factor before matching its meaning

LOOKS-LIKE-COSTAR already gives the same digit different roles: Life Path, Soul Urge, Expression, first-name flavor, called-name perception and full-name direction. It would lose information to collapse those to one “dominant8.” Compare internal motivation with the relevant needs/value context; compare outward presentation with expression/relational context; compare long-term direction with purpose and public engagement. These are proposed role-aware comparisons, not permission to say Soul Urge equals Moon or called name equals Ascendant. Mystic's AC/MC/DC/IC meanings supply a richer emergence/public-output/relational-intake/inner-anchoring axis, with the explicit limit that MC is not necessarily H10. [Anchors](https://github.com/olsonan26/mystic-rebels-chart-generator/blob/3d83db75c30b26d9b068576d84ec49554822fd8c/data/knowledge/interpretive_inventory.json#L3108), LOOKS-LIKE-COSTAR `name-engine/data/numbers.ts:4–35`.

### 2. PMEI suggests functional comparisons rather than four-element substitution

PMEI defines Physical as output/body/environment, Mental as thinking/planning/research, Emotional as mood/bonding/creative drive, and Intuitive as sensing/subtext/pattern feel. That supports a four-axis comparison with multiple Mystic components per axis: Physical with Mars and tangible Taurus/Virgo expression; Mental with Mercury and structured Saturn condition; Emotional with Moon, Venus and Sun's creative themes; Intuitive with Neptune, Pluto's hidden material and relevant Pisces context. No inspected source authorizes “Physical=Earth, Mental=Air, Emotional=Water, Intuitive=Fire.” The PMEI bins themselves put4/5 in Physical,1/8 Mental,2/3/6 Emotional and7/9 Intuitive; those numeric bins are not astrological elements. Preserve the four PMEI counts as counts. LOOKS-LIKE-COSTAR `services/pmeiEngine.ts:19–61`, [Mystic planet vocabulary](https://github.com/olsonan26/mystic-rebels-chart-generator/blob/3d83db75c30b26d9b068576d84ec49554822fd8c/docs/PROFILE_REPORT_KNOWLEDGE_PATH.md#L251-L260).

### 3. A zero PMEI plane and an empty house make an instructive comparison

Both systems explicitly resist interpreting absence in the diagram as absence of a life capacity. LOOKS-LIKE-COSTAR treats zero planes as an all-or-nothing switch; Mystic says an empty house still has its Field, sign and ruler context. This is a shared interpretive caution and a useful report-design principle. It does not establish that a zero plane causes an empty house, or that both absence types have identical meaning. LOOKS-LIKE-COSTAR `services/pmeiEngine.ts:11,206–212,315–316`, [empty house concept](https://github.com/olsonan26/mystic-rebels-chart-generator/blob/3d83db75c30b26d9b068576d84ec49554822fd8c/docs/PROFILE_REPORT_KNOWLEDGE_PATH.md#L276).

### 4. Build a personal-versus-collective lens from the actual house groupings

Mystic's documented angular triads are **Self12/1/2; Personal3/4/5; Others6/7/8; World9/10/11**. They are distinct from both temporal zodiac quadrants and physical Vitality Anchor quadrants. This offers a more nuanced way to investigate whether a number's themes are expressed inwardly, in close personal life, in one-to-one exchange, or publicly/collectively. Number9's humanitarian vocabulary, for example, could be examined against a World grouping without reducing it to H9. The complete contextual weighting is not implemented, and raw planet counts should not be advertised as importance scores. [Original assertion at3632](https://github.com/olsonan26/mystic-rebels-chart-generator/blob/3d83db75c30b26d9b068576d84ec49554822fd8c/data/knowledge/original_assertions.json#L3632), [separate coordinate frames at3676](https://github.com/olsonan26/mystic-rebels-chart-generator/blob/3d83db75c30b26d9b068576d84ec49554822fd8c/data/knowledge/original_assertions.json#L3676).

### 5. Process styles: mutable, cardinal and fixed

The source explicitly teaches Mutable → Cardinal → Fixed, allows entry anywhere and chooses Gemini pedagogically. Numerological5's adaptation,1's initiation and4's sustained building suggest a useful candidate process comparison. These are not equivalences: the full qualities of all numbers and signs remain necessary, and the source's teaching sequence does not rotate coordinates or require one starting node. [Original assertion at3561](https://github.com/olsonan26/mystic-rebels-chart-generator/blob/3d83db75c30b26d9b068576d84ec49554822fd8c/data/knowledge/original_assertions.json#L3561).

### 6. “With versus while” is an excellent cross-system evidence discipline

Mystic distinguishes directly connected astrological factors from concurrently present factors. That supplies a useful conceptual safeguard: two systems mentioning responsibility may be parallel observations, but they do not acquire a physical aspect, causal link or statistical confirmation merely by appearing together. In a future bridge, label an authored thematic correspondence separately from a calculated astrological contact and from an empirically tested association. The source itself discusses WITH/WHILE within astrology; extending it to numerology is our design proposal. [Concept and implementation gap](https://github.com/olsonan26/mystic-rebels-chart-generator/blob/3d83db75c30b26d9b068576d84ec49554822fd8c/docs/PROFILE_REPORT_KNOWLEDGE_PATH.md#L301), retained PDF450–451 independently read.

### 7. Node integration can support a growth dialogue, with limits

Both repositories retain a “use both ends” approach rather than abandoning the South Node. A numerological autonomy-versus-cooperation contrast could be compared to an Aries/Libra node-axis theme; care-versus-achievement could be compared to Cancer/Capricorn. However, Life Path must not be declared North Node, nor a repeated name digit South Node. Mystic also preserves mean/true node distinctions and ordering that a generic node template can omit. The copied framework's literal past-mastery or soul claims should remain attributed philosophy rather than known biography. [Mystic nodes concept](https://github.com/olsonan26/mystic-rebels-chart-generator/blob/3d83db75c30b26d9b068576d84ec49554822fd8c/data/knowledge/interpretive_inventory.json#L2905), [mean/true order assertion](https://github.com/olsonan26/mystic-rebels-chart-generator/blob/3d83db75c30b26d9b068576d84ec49554822fd8c/data/knowledge/original_assertions.json#L4987), LOOKS-LIKE-COSTAR `alchemistic_astrology_knowledge_base/core_frameworks/nodes_integration.ts`.

### 8. Convergence and tension are both useful research outcomes

A4 emphasis alongside grounded, structured chart components is candidate reinforcement. A4 emphasis alongside5-like exploration or change themes is candidate tension or complementarity. An emotionally dominant PMEI profile with analytically expressed placements is not a failed chart: it suggests comparing sensitivity, decision method and communication as separate functions. A report should preserve the disagreement instead of forcing every factor into one flattering story. Mystic's Block/Mix-n-Match/Color method explicitly calls for contextual composition, but its colors do not authorize numerical weights. [Block/Color](https://github.com/olsonan26/mystic-rebels-chart-generator/blob/3d83db75c30b26d9b068576d84ec49554822fd8c/data/knowledge/interpretive_inventory.json#L1344).

## Boundaries relevant to implementation

- Spark, the full directional aspect network and progressions remain blocked in `mystic_rebels/service.py:22–24`; Sophia remains unavailable in `catalog.py`. Partial source recovery does not supply a complete executable rule.
- Natal positions, Whole Sign Field, Tone, Pulse, modern ruler lookup and fixed Fortune/Spirit/Love formulas exist. Unknown birth time suppresses houses/angles and house-derived placements; it must also suppress any bridge that needs them. [Service](https://github.com/olsonan26/mystic-rebels-chart-generator/blob/3d83db75c30b26d9b068576d84ec49554822fd8c/mystic_rebels/service.py#L39).
- A mathematical point receives influence in the source; `outgoing_emission:false` must not become a zero-radius emitter. Numerological equality must not manufacture an astrological aspect. [Directional constraints](https://github.com/olsonan26/mystic-rebels-chart-generator/blob/3d83db75c30b26d9b068576d84ec49554822fd8c/docs/METHODOLOGY_SOURCE_REVIEW.md#L86-L94).
- Source-backed sign and Chiron meanings replace generic-library meanings. Chiron is Fractured Structures; Eunomia has two unresolved alternatives that cannot be merged. [Meaning-use guide](https://github.com/olsonan26/mystic-rebels-chart-generator/blob/3d83db75c30b26d9b068576d84ec49554822fd8c/docs/REPORT_COMPONENT_USE.md#L1).
- A Life Path and natal chart both depend on birth date; multiple name metrics share letters. Repeated matches are not independent confirmations. A numerical correlation study would need predeclared features, suitable controls, independent holdout people, birth-time uncertainty and adjustment for the many candidate comparisons. No such cross-system result was established in this audit.

The strongest first integration is a **static natal/name comparison with a source ledger**: keep each factor's calculation, role, meaning, context, provenance and uncertainty; show candidate shared themes and tensions; leave daily timelines optional. This can be built and evaluated without enabling unresolved Spark, progressions or the full directional aspect engine. A complete personal report still requires the separately missing contextual assembly and approval work.
