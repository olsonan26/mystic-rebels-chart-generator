## Findings that change the knowledge model

1. **Labels do not provide meanings.** PDF283-284 requires planet, house, degree, decan, aspects, sign and ruler context. PDF290's Mercury5/15/25 Aries examples hold Tone constant while Pulse changes; these simplified illustrations still omit full personal context. Returning the label fields is not implementation of the reading.
2. **A ruler name is not a Delegate.** PDF258-259 carries ruled-sign occupants, their condition and house Field forward. Venus in ninth-house Aries brings that context through Mars. Preserve cycles and multiple responsibilities; do not flatten to a final dispositor.
3. **Retrograde belongs to distinct layers.** PDF271-273 separates an Rx occupant from a house carried through an Rx ruler. It rejects contagious retrograde and requires sequential double-retrograde reading. It names modern-ruler H4/H5 template exceptions. Physical velocity and interpreted house condition need different fields.
4. **Directed edges require complete receivers.** PDF322,324 requires the receiver's placement, ruler and relationships. PDF345 distinguishes Mars-before-Venus from Venus-before-Mars. PDF297,457 forbids summed OOI across ecosystems and allows one-way contacts. A symmetric aspect or generic compatibility phrase loses this information.
5. **No contact is not no context.** PDF449-451 includes pass-through and concurrent WHILE pressures. Graph traversal alone cannot select all relevant context. An absent/blocked aspect engine also cannot prove a non-aspect.
6. **Parts carry an interpretation contract.** PDF547-559 rejects night reversal, automatic Fortune/luck and Love/romance meanings. First interpret how the ordered formula triggers, then how its point expresses. PDF550-551 describes exact activation; PDF554 discusses a distinct incoming natal relationship. Do not collapse these into a universal zero-orb aspect or generic receiving orb.
7. **Point coordinates omit substantial content.** PDF521-522 describes anchor-based purpose;526-534 distinguishes Vertex visibility/overextension from Invertex reset;535-537 adds Axial Flow. PDF539-544 distinguishes Nonagesimal potential from MC/IC. These source meanings are absent from runtime.
8. **Additional bodies need their own reviewed semantics.** Chiron emphasizes structural difference rather than automatic wounded-healer language. Juno concerns conditions of commitment beyond romance; Ceres sustenance/survival; Fama broadcast versus reception. The 40 asteroid records preserve distinctions and qualify fragment/comparison-only support.
9. **Context changes with time and geography.** PDF430-435 layers progressed/natal/relocated identity. PDF452 calls for eight chart contexts in full Synastry. PDF636-640 distinguishes horizon/Paran events from ecliptic angles;648-650 distinguishes Local Space origins and travel. The natal request schema is not an implementation of these workflows.
10. **Accuracy includes faithful language and user agency.** PDF135-140 and405-406 translates grounded themes into personal language without inventing facts. PDF671 warns against dependence on consulting astrology for every decision. Daily usefulness should support choice. Author claims of certainty, medical inference or scientific proof are not inherited as verified product claims.
