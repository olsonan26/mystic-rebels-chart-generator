### Material findings beyond the earlier summaries

1. **The explicit chapter order and lexicon order differ.** Printed 216 says Degree -> Decan -> Aspects -> Sign. Lexicon u says Degree -> Decan -> Sign -> Aspect. The requested chapter sequence is supported; preserve the lexicon variant in the evidence packet. Its presence only in a research document is insufficient when the writer receives a smaller pack.
2. **The reverse transit example contains an unregistered mismatch.** On original printed 503, scenario B makes Planet A the natal Sun in H5 and places the transit Moon in H6, but its aspect paragraph retains an H4 reference. Preserve this conflicting source row rather than silently correcting it. The compiled text also fails to preserve that reverse example as a complete coherent passage/diagram sequence. The full observation and exact original hash are in the source audit.
3. **Much of the missing answer mechanism is already described in stored sources.** Printed 327-328 requires the receiving participant's placement, ruler and other aspects before constructing practical resolution. Printed 497-499 requires the current sky's own condition before its personal overlay. Printed 527-529 distinguishes direct contact, transit-only reach, ruler/pass-through context and secondary thematic presence. A symmetric aspect label or two keywords cannot perform these steps.
4. **The runtime rule pack is incomplete even as a source packet.** Its 32 concepts and 13 profiles match their recorded current input hashes, but `planet-lexicon` is missing from dependency closure. Linked original assertions, blocker findings, full requirement context and the lexicon variation are absent. `objective-context` and `agency` are also relevant to open questions. The canonical portable topic packets are a better starting authority than a second small hand-selected glossary.
5. **The existing transit equality scanner has demonstrated completeness failures.** Synthetic same-day paired crossings and a real Mercury station touch are missed by its endpoint sign-change search. Matching daily/six-hour contact dates does not establish a complete event search. Existing identified crossings are not invalidated by that finding; the algorithm's general completeness is.
6. **Old statements about missing asteroid meanings are stale.** Flora, Ixion, Nessus and Sedna meanings have since been recovered into stored source records. Their contextual use still needs implementation. Do not redo acquisition or substitute generic meanings on the assumption that those sources are absent.

## A concrete consultation flow

1. Select or enter the person's chart. Retain birth-time uncertainty and the exact chart fingerprint.
2. Type the question and optional reported circumstances. Select Now or an explicit date/time/zone and, where needed, an interval. Keep reported context separate from facts calculated from the chart.
3. Form an inspectable evidence plan: intended question, relevant chart factors, selection reasons, required source concepts and layers. Different questions must not always receive the same Chiron narrative or one of four canned answers.
4. Retrieve the full applicable source dependency graph, assertions, exclusions, competing witnesses and unresolved findings. Carry source/rule versions into the calculation and answer packets.
5. Assess each relevant natal factor through PHDDASR. Continue through actual rulers, occupants and receiving condition with cycle references. Keep sign ruler, decan ruler, house Delegate and a plotted natal planet distinct. Preserve separate physical and delegated retrograde conditions.
6. For current questions, calculate the current sky independently, then qualified contacts to fixed natal positions. Carry current and natal condition separately. Treat thematic pass-through, direct contact and concurrent unrelated influences as different relations.
7. Return a direct answer to the question with a constructive option and its actual limits. An explanation view exposes the seven stages, relevant chart facts, exact epochs, contacts, missing prerequisites and sources. Missing degree/aspect rules limit specific claims; they must not be reported as completed stages.
8. Save a versioned consultation packet containing question/context, chart and source versions, calculation epochs, answer and revisions in recoverable files. A later refresh creates a dated revision and preserves the earlier answer.

For example, a relationship question should select and explain its relevant relationship/communication/boundary evidence rather than infer the situation from a natal label. A timing question additionally requires an explicitly dated contact and applicable timing rule. Source explanation questions can be answered directly from reviewed passages without pretending to calculate a personal forecast.

## Implementation order and acceptance gates

| Work | Completion evidence |
|---|---|
| Source packet repair | Full dependency closure; linked contradictions and assertions included; selected chapter order and lexicon variation retained; stale hashes rejected; precise per-claim references |
| Current sky view | Now/date picker, explicit resolved UTC/local zone, real positions/motion and unavailable reasons; main chart's natal data preserved |
| Personal overlay | Current sky shown over fixed natal sectors; natal and transit rulers/conditions separated; current location used only for a documented relevant layer |
| Geometry/event service | Stations split monotonic arcs; wrap, tangencies, paired crossings, endpoints and all repeat passes tested; entry/exact/closest approach/exit separately typed |
| Directed source rules | Per-body reach, angle caps, ecosystem constraints and equality/hand-off/Fringe rules selected only where supported; unknown cases retained explicitly |
| First typed-question topic | Real question input, documented relevance selection, complete prerequisites for each actual claim, readable response, explanation, save/export/reopen and adversarial writer checks |
| Broader forecasts | Progression epoch/angles, relocation coupling, activation calendar and any delay/return conventions verified before those claims are enabled |

The first four deliverables can make the chart useful for current research while source interpretation gaps are resolved. The full directed-aspect and receiving-condition layer is the major prerequisite for claiming a complete PHDDASR answer. The remaining 304-image queue should be prioritized by these dependencies, diagrams, boundaries and contradictions, rather than simple image order.
